Privacy

Last updated 23 September 2026

xrp.bsvkey.com is a static site: an overview of the BSVKey XRP rail and an in-browser XRP wallet tool. It is built to collect as little as possible, and it has no server of its own.

Your keys stay in your browser

Wallets you create or import on the wallet page are generated and held in your browser. Your seed and secret key are never sent to us, and we have no way to see, store, recover, or reset them.

If you tick Save in this browser, the seed is kept in this browser's local storage on this device only, unencrypted, until you use Forget wallet. Backup files you export (.json or .txt) contain the seed in plain text and are saved wherever you choose. Anyone with your seed, a backup file, or unlocked access to your device can spend your funds.

Who the page talks to

The wallet page connects only to public XRP Ledger nodes, and a Content-Security-Policy blocks every other destination:

  • Mainnet: xrplcluster.com, to read balances and submit transactions you send.
  • Testnet: Ripple's public testnet node and testnet faucet, when you choose testnet or request test XRP.

Those services are operated by others. Like any server, they see your IP address and the account addresses you look up or fund, under their own policies. Anything you send on the XRP Ledger, including amounts, addresses, destination tags, and memos, is public and permanent. Do not put personal information in a memo.

What we do not collect

No accounts, no sign-in, no forms, no cookies, no analytics, and no third-party trackers. The page's code, including its cryptographic libraries, is served from this site rather than a third-party CDN.

Hosting

The site is hosted by Netlify, which processes standard request data (such as IP address, user agent, and the page requested) to serve the site and protect it. We do not use that data to build profiles.

Email

If you email us (for example through a Request early access link), we receive what you send and use it only to reply. We do not sell or share it.

Other sites

Links to other BSVKey sites, GitHub, and XRP Ledger services lead to pages with their own policies.
